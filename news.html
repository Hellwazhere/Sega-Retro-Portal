30 Years of History (1996 - 2026)

2024 - 2025

The Analogue 3D Era

Hardware-level FPGA recreation becomes the gold standard, moving away from software emulation to "real" hardware feel.

2018 - 2020

The Mini-Console Boom

Sega releases the Mega Drive Mini, sparking a global wave of official plug-and-play nostalgia.

2012 - 2015

Indie Dreamcast Revival

Commercial indie releases like *Sturmwind* prove the Dreamcast scene is immortal, over a decade after its death.

2006 - 2008

The Virtual Console Launch

Nintendo and Sega partner to bring classic Mega Drive titles to the Wii, ending the era of purely physical retro gaming.

2001

The Great Shift

Sega announces its exit from hardware; the "Retro" era for Sega begins as they become a multi-platform developer.

1998 - 1999

Bleem! & The Emulation Wars

The first commercial emulators appear, leading to massive legal battles and the birth of the modern emulation scene.

1996

The Dawn of 3D

*Nights into Dreams* launches on Saturn, showcasing the potential of 3D movement with the first analog controller.
