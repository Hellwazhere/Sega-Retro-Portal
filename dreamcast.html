The Future That Arrived Early

After the failure of the Saturn, Sega poured everything into the Dreamcast. It launched on 9/9/99—becoming one of the most successful launch days in retail history.

Online Pioneer

The first console with a built-in 56k modem. Games like Phantasy Star Online changed the landscape forever.

The VMU

The Visual Memory Unit featured a tiny LCD screen for second-screen info—a concept decades ahead of its time.

The Demise

Despite its brilliance, it faced the unstoppable PS2 hype. In January 2001, Sega announced its exit from hardware.
