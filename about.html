About

Digital Preservation

In the rapidly evolving landscape of interactive media, history is often at risk of being lost to time. Digital Preservation is our commitment to ensuring that the legacy of Sega, from the 16-bit "Blast Processing" of the Genesis to the visionary internet-ready Dreamcast remains accessible for future generations. We don't just archive files, we safeguard the art, code, and documentation that defined a cultural era. By fighting hardware obsolescence and "bit rot," this portal serves as a permanent sanctuary for the innovations that moved the industry forward.

Why Sega Retro

This site is a focused archive of Sega history, built to spotlight the hardware, the culture, and the key moments that shaped the medium. The goal is simple: keep the best stories easy to find, accurate, and enjoyable to explore.
